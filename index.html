Neden Böyle? Shorts Creator

Neden Böyle? Shorts Creator is a personal, locally run tool that produces short, sourced science videos in Turkish and publishes them to the creator's own YouTube channel, Neden Böyle? (@nedenboylekisa).

What the app does

  • Prepares short educational videos from public scientific sources and free stock footage.
  • Uploads approved videos to the creator's own YouTube channel and schedules their publication time.
  • Adds each uploaded video to a matching playlist on the same channel and posts a question comment under it.
  • Reads the channel's own YouTube Analytics to measure which topics perform well.

The app is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to other users and does not access any channel other than the one the owner signs in with.
