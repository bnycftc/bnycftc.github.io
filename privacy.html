Privacy Policy

Neden Böyle? Shorts Creator · Last updated: 4 October 2026

Neden Böyle? Shorts Creator ("the app") is a personal tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Neden Böyle?.

Google and YouTube data the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests the following permissions for the owner's own YouTube channel:

  • youtube.upload – to upload videos the owner has approved.
  • youtube.readonly – to read the channel name, its videos, playlists and upload status.
  • yt-analytics.readonly – to read the channel's own performance statistics (views, watch time, retention).
  • youtube.force-ssl – to add the owner's uploaded videos to the owner's playlists and to post a comment under the owner's own videos.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload, schedule and organise the owner's own videos and to measure their performance for the owner.
  • OAuth tokens and analytics snapshots are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app; there is no such server.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access. Locally stored tokens and data can be deleted by disconnecting YouTube in the app or deleting the app's data folder. For any request, contact the owner via the channel page youtube.com/@nedenboylekisa.
